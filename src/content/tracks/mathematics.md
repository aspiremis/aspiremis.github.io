Most power system texts open with "recall that…" and then use something I
half-remember from second-year undergrad. This track is where I stop
half-remembering.

Scoped tightly: not a general maths course, only the parts that show up — phasor
algebra, eigenvalues for stability, sparse linear systems, and enough
probability to read a stochastic hosting-capacity paper properly.
