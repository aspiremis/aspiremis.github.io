The MATLAB track covers *how* to call a solver. This one covers why it fails.

Split out deliberately: conditioning and convergence are language-independent
ideas, and learning them as MATLAB trivia rather than as mathematics is exactly
how I ended up confused about fast decoupled load flow in my first month.
