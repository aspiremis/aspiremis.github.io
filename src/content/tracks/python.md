MATLAB is what the coursework speaks. Python is what most published research
tooling speaks, and it is free, which matters once the campus licence goes away.

This track deliberately comes second. Learning arrays in two languages
simultaneously is a good way to internalise neither, and several ideas —
vectorisation, broadcasting, floating point — are far easier the second time.
The final module is a direct MATLAB-to-Python translation guide, which is only
useful once both halves are real.
