Every course this semester quietly assumes MATLAB. Nobody teaches it, and the
tutorials I found either explain syntax with no engineering context, or jump
straight to a power system example with no explanation of the language.

This track is the thing I wanted and couldn't find. It starts from *why a
language like MATLAB exists at all*, and every concept is motivated by a problem
I actually have — per-unit conversion, building a Y-bus, sweeping a load profile
— rather than by the order a language reference happens to list features in.
