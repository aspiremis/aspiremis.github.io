The tracks above teach tools. This one is about the problems those tools exist
to solve, and it assumes you've done the work in at least one of them.

Planned rather than written: it depends on the MATLAB numerical-methods module
landing first, because implementing a load flow before understanding sparsity
and conditioning produces code that works on a 5-bus system and falls over on a
realistic one.
