## The plan

Phase I is where a vague interest has to become a question that can be answered
in a year. My current direction — subject to change once I have more of Semester 2
behind me — sits at the intersection of distribution-level PV integration and
data-driven analysis:

> How far can measurement-driven methods substitute for full time-series
> simulation when estimating PV hosting capacity on unbalanced distribution
> feeders?

The appeal is that both halves are things I want to be good at anyway, and the
answer is genuinely useful to a distribution utility rather than only to a paper.

## Milestones I'm setting myself

| Month | Target |
|---|---|
| July 2027 | Scope fixed, ~40 papers read and catalogued |
| August 2027 | Reproduce a published hosting-capacity result end to end |
| September 2027 | Baseline method implemented and validated |
| October 2027 | First set of comparative results |
| November 2027 | Phase I report and seminar |

Reproducing someone else's published result before attempting my own is a
deliberate first step. If I can't reproduce a known number, I have no business
trusting a new one.
