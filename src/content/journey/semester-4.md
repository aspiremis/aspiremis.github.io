## What "done" should look like

A thesis that is only a document is a thesis that stops existing the day it is
submitted. I would like this one to leave behind three things:

1. **The document itself** — defended and archived.
2. **A released, documented tool.** If the method is worth anything, someone else
   should be able to run it on their own feeder without emailing me first.
3. **A paper.** Even a conference paper forces the argument to survive contact
   with reviewers who have no reason to be generous.

## After this

Two directions I'm weighing, and don't need to resolve yet:

- **Industry** — grid integration studies, distribution planning, or power system
  software. The work is real, the feedback loop is short, and India's distribution
  sector has a great deal of unglamorous, high-impact problems in it.
- **PhD** — if Phase II leaves me with more open questions than closed ones, and
  the right group and problem line up.

I'd rather choose that on the evidence of two years of work than on a preference
I hold today.
